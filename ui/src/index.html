Model tier

Strong
Medium
Cheap
Strong — flagship reasoning models (o3, Opus 4.8, Gemini 3.1 Pro, GPT-5.5 Pro). Best answers on hard exam questions. Slow (30 s – 10 min per answer for deep reasoning) and expensive (up to $180 per 1M output tokens). Use for the hardest MCQs or when Medium keeps missing.
Medium — balanced flagships (GPT-5.5, Sonnet 5, Gemini 3.5 Flash). Sub-10 s answers on typical exam questions, still highly accurate. Costs ~10× less than Strong. Recommended default.
Cheap — small/fast (GPT-5-mini, Haiku 4.5, Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite). ~2 s answers, cheap enough for study drills. Good for factual recall, weaker on multi-step math/logic.
